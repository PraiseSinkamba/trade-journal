# Sketch 001: Contextual AI assistant

## Design Question
How should users converse with AI inside a workspace while retaining clear, user-confirmed control over proposed journal changes?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/001-contextual-ai-assistant/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants
- **A: Persistent copilot** — a durable side-by-side conversation and draft panel.
- **B: Focused side sheet** — AI opens on demand over the contextual workspace.
- **C: Inline assist** — lightweight AI prompts and draft cards live in the record itself.

## What to Look For
Compare whether each option feels native to trade review, preserves enough room for the source record, and makes draft-only status and the handoff to existing forms unmistakable.
